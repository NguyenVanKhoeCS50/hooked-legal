Hooked — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Hooked ("the app", "we") helps anglers plan trips, check fishing conditions, save spots, log catches and use AI-assisted fishing features. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. Device permissions and regional advertising choices are requested separately where applicable.

1. Accounts and how we identify you

You can use Hooked without creating an account; an anonymous profile is created so features such as AI and subscriptions can work. You may sign in with Apple or Google. When you do, we store an account identifier and the name and email address provided by your sign-in provider. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

2. Location

Hooked uses your location only after you grant permission. It is used to center the map, calculate nearby weather, tide and fishing conditions, find nearby community catches and save spots where you choose. Coordinates are sent to our cloud functions to fetch forecasts and are not used to build a location history. Saved spots and catch logs are stored on your device.

3. Photos and AI features

When you ask the AI assistant a question or choose a photo for fish identification, the photo, your message, your fishing profile (such as target species and experience) and current conditions are sent over an encrypted connection through Google Firebase and our cloud functions to OpenAI to generate an answer. The identification photo is processed to answer your request and is not stored in our image storage. AI conversations may be saved with your account so you can see your history. Do not include personal or sensitive information in photos or messages. AI answers may be wrong; see the Terms of Service.

4. Community

Sharing to the Community is optional and happens only when you tap Post. You need a signed-in account to post, react, report or block.

  • What others see: your angler name, the photo, species, optional length, weight, bait and caption, the time, and an approximate area. By default the area is a grid square of about 2 km with the nearest town and province names. For each post you can choose to show only the province, or to hide the location.
  • Your exact spot stays private. If location is available, your device sends the coordinate once with the post. Our server immediately reduces it to the approximate area and does not store or show the exact coordinate. Photo metadata, including embedded GPS data, is removed before the photo is stored.
  • Area suggestions: to suggest which fish are caught nearby, we count species per area of about 100 km and per month. These counts contain no names, photos or account identifiers.
  • Safety: before a post is published, its photo and text are checked by an automated moderation service (OpenAI). Posts can be reported; a post reported by several people is hidden until reviewed. We may remove content that breaks the community guidelines.
  • Your controls: you can delete your posts at any time and block other anglers. Deleting your account removes your posts, photos, reactions, reports and blocks.

5. Purchases

Hooked Pro is sold through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We never see or store your payment details. We receive purchase verification and subscription status and record it against your account so Pro features work.

6. Permissions

  • Location (optional) — maps, nearby conditions, nearby community catches and saving spots.
  • Camera (optional) — only when you take a catch photo.
  • Selected photos (optional) — on iOS, the system photo picker shares only the images you choose.
  • Notifications (optional) — fish activity reminders for the forecast location you select.

7. Advertising and tracking

The free app shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, usage information and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. We do not send your photos, catch logs, spots or AI messages to AdMob. Where required, including the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Google User Messaging Platform asks for your advertising choices, and you can revisit them in Settings. The iOS app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the device advertising identifier (IDFA). Hooked Pro removes ads.

8. Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud functions, database and image storage for Community posts. OpenAI processes AI requests and content moderation. Google Maps provides maps. Weather, marine and tide data come from forecast providers through our cloud functions. Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform provide advertising and privacy choices. Apple and Google process store purchases. These providers may process data outside your country under their own privacy terms.

Provider information: Firebase, OpenAI, Google (Maps), and Google advertising.

9. Data retention and your rights

Account data and Community posts are kept while your account is active. Use Profile → Delete account to delete your account and associated cloud data, or contact us for help. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; manage that in the App Store or Google Play. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app. Provider security logs and backups may remain for their retention periods. Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR / CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data; email us to exercise them. We do not sell personal information.

10. Children

Hooked is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
